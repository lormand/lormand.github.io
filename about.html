About

lormand is a public showcase for stills and motion out of Austin. The work lives on canyon roads and lake weather — sports cars, motorcycles, the two-lane that everyone already knows if they know Lime Creek.

This is not a biography and not a client deck. Motion is published on YouTube. Stills on this site are web-sized; file watermarks from NAS masters can land later. The corner mark you see on stills is CSS for v1.

Lime Creek Road is the first series: a weekly canyon shoot. More series drop in as folders, not as a redesign.
